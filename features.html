01 CAPTURE

Start with the exact pixels.

SnapDog gives you a precision loupe for tight region selections, plus the speed of a menu bar hotkey when you already know what you need.

  • Read live coordinates in the 4× loupe and the width × height badge

  • Arrows, shapes, text, speech balloons, and numbered steps
  • Blur, pixelate, spotlight, and highlight tools
  • Crop, straighten, resize, undo, and editable objects

01 / CAPTURE

Get the shot

  • Full screen, window, and region capture with rebindable hotkeys.
  • Precision region picker with 4× loupe, live coordinates, and a width × height badge.
  • Window picker with hover highlight and app and window names, including dialogs and utility panels.
  • Multiple displays: capture under the cursor or choose a display first.
  • Delay by 3, 5, or 10 seconds with a cancellable countdown.
  • Stitch tall pages: choose a region, scroll, and add frames with overlap alignment.
  • Recapture Last repeats the most recent capture mode.
  • Camera snapshot opens a preview before the shutter.
02 / SCREEN TOOLS

Read and measure

  • Recognize Text in a selected region with Apple Vision; copy or save the result.
  • Decode QR and other barcodes from a region or an existing image.
  • Generate a QR code and copy the PNG.
  • Pick Color with a magnifier and live hex and RGB values.
  • Screen ruler reports distance, horizontal and vertical offsets, and angle; Shift snaps direction.
03 / EDITOR

Make it clear

  • Annotation tools: arrows, lines, freehand strokes, text, speech balloons, step numbers, highlights, spotlights, blur, and pixelate.
  • Shapes: rectangles, rounded rectangles, ellipses, triangles, diamonds, pentagons, hexagons, stars, and polygons.
  • Edit after drawing: select objects to change color, fill, opacity, stroke, and size.
  • Objects panel to select, reorder, and delete annotations.
  • Image transforms: crop, straighten, resize, rotate, zoom, and pan.
  • Undo, redo, and duplicate while you work.
  • Watermark on canvas that you can move and resize.
  • Export to PNG, JPEG, TIFF, or HEIC with quality controls.
04 / RECORDING

Show the whole story

  • Record a screen, window, or region to MP4 or MOV, export a GIF, or make a webcam-only clip.
  • Screen + Camera one-off mode or a draggable, resizable webcam picture-in-picture overlay.
  • Pause and resume without a gap in the saved recording.
  • System and microphone audio options, recorded as separate tracks.
  • Click ripples baked into walkthrough recordings.
  • Countdown and display picker before recording begins.
  • Quality controls: High, Balanced, Small, or a custom bitrate; Automatic, 4K H.264, HEVC, and ProRes recording modes.
  • Extra GIF export after recording or from a saved video in History.
05 / AI & TRANSCRIPTS

Turn pixels into information

  • Built-in on-device analysis describes a capture, extracts text, or suggests a filename with no account or model download.
  • Optional provider profiles for cloud services or local models such as Ollama and LM Studio.
  • On-device speech transcription for recordings, with automatic transcription available after every save.
  • Transcript actions in History: transcribe, view, or summarize with your configured provider.
  • Profile-scoped Keychain storage for provider credentials.
06 / EFFECTS & OUTPUT

Finish it on save

  • Beautify frames with a gradient, rounded corners, and a soft shadow.
  • Image effects including resize-before-save, border, drop shadow, and movable text watermark.
  • Filename templates with date, time, name, capture source, and persistent sequence tokens.
  • Retina suffix option for output filenames.
  • Choose your default image and video formats and recording quality.
07 / AUTOMATION

Make the next step automatic

  • Visual workflow builder connects capture triggers, OCR, AI, actions, and results.
  • Workflow hotkeys launch your own capture chains with per-workflow behavior.
  • Post-capture actions for upload, webhook, Slack, and AI, with reorderable steps.
  • Default behavior: save locally, run actions, or ask every time.
  • After-capture switches for save, copy, open in editor, pin, upload, and transcript.
  • Watch folder imports new images and videos from a folder you choose.
  • Webhooks on save or upload, with HMAC-SHA256 signing and retries.
08 / SHARING

Use your destination

  • 18 built-in upload and handoff options: Catbox.moe, 0x0.st, Imgur, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, Amazon S3 and compatible hosts, SFTP, Slack, Discord, Telegram, Notion, Mastodon, Bluesky, Cloudinary, GitHub, iCloud Drive, and AirDrop.
  • ShareX custom uploaders imported from .sxcu files.
  • URL shortening with is.gd or an imported ShareX shortener.
  • Auto-upload or upload on demand, then copy the resulting link.
  • Destination test, recent uploads, and remote delete where supported.
  • Copy for publishing as Markdown image, Markdown link, HTML, or BBCode.
09 / HISTORY

Find it and use it again

  • Search and filter captures, recordings, sources, tags, and favorites.

  • Batch delete and export an HTML gallery.
  • Pin to Screen as a floating, resizable reference you can drag into another app.
  • Recent Captures panel for the last five shots.

Fit your Mac workflow

  • Custom global hotkeys for capture modes and tools.
  • Optional macOS shortcut takeover for the familiar screenshot key combinations.
  • Launch at login and choose a color or monochrome menu bar icon.
  • Capture preferences for shutter sound, window shadow, region preview, and multi-display behavior.
  • Camera controls for the draggable preview and mirror setting.
  • Localized in 13 languages, including English, Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, and more.
11 / PRIVACY

Local unless you decide otherwise

  • No SnapDog account and no app usage analytics.
  • Captures stay local until you choose an upload or configure an automatic destination.
  • On-device OCR and transcription use Apple frameworks.
  • Optional provider requests go to the provider you configure.
  • Secrets in Keychain, including provider keys, OAuth tokens, and upload credentials.
  • Just-in-time permissions for screen recording, camera, and microphone.
